Overview

A remote-controlled 5-axis robotic arm. The mechanical structure is a 3D-printed open-source model sourced from Thingiverse, which I printed and assembled. The engineering work went into everything that drives it.

I designed the control electronics and the embedded logic from scratch: servo driving, the Bluetooth link for teleoperation, and the firmware coordinating the five axes. This is why the breakdown rates mechanical difficulty low and electronics and software high.
